Internal stimulus pre-test

You will listen to pairs of short sounds: a reference (Sound A) and a modified version (Sound B).

You will compare their acoustic characteristics and judge what changes in a person's mental state they might represent in an auditory biofeedback system.

What do the sounds communicate? We are asking what the sounds seem to represent about another person's mental state, rather than whether they change your own mental state. The separate sound-quality questions ask how annoying or pleasant you find the sound itself.

Set up your listening environment

  • Use headphones if possible, in a quiet environment.
  • Play the volume-check sound below and choose a comfortable volume.
  • Keep that volume unchanged throughout the experiment.
  • Avoid interruptions and give your own independent judgments.
