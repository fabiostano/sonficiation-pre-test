Before you begin

You will complete 40 comparisons. Each comparison has two short steps:

  1. Listen to Sound A, then Sound B. Rate the mental-state changes Sound B seems to communicate relative to Sound A, followed by its annoyance and pleasantness.
  2. Compare one acoustic characteristic of Sound B with Sound A using your own numerical estimate.

Your first ratings are saved before the acoustic characteristic is revealed. You may replay the sounds as often as needed, including on the second step.

100
Sound A always has a reference value of 100.

This is a perceptual reference, rather than the physical setting of the sound.

For Sound B, enter a positive number: below 100 means less of the characteristic, 100 means no perceived difference, and above 100 means more. Decimals are allowed. There is no upper response limit.

For mental-state ratings, the seven positions range from −3 to +3; 0 means no perceived change in the mental state communicated. Please actively choose each response.

There are no correct or incorrect answers. Respond according to your own perception. Short optional breaks are offered during the experiment.
